Our Story

About Brother LPG Gas Plant

Born from a family commitment to safe and affordable energy, Brother LPG has grown from a single filling station into one of Pakistan's most trusted LPG distributors.

Est. 1999

A Quarter Century of Fueling Pakistan

Founded in 1999 by Muhammad Aslam in Karachi, Brother LPG began as a modest cylinder refilling unit serving the local community. The founding principle was simple: deliver gas safely, on time, and at a fair price.

Within a decade, we expanded to serve commercial kitchens, textile factories, and hospitals across Sindh. Today, with a fleet of 40 tanker trucks, 3 processing plants, and over 300 employees, we serve 50+ districts nationwide.

Our ISO 9001:2015 certification and consistent safety record reflect our non-negotiable commitment to quality — a standard set by our founder that every team member upholds daily.
