Our Story

About Brother LPG — an LPG supplier in Pakistan

We started as a small cylinder refill shop. Today Brother LPG supplies cooking gas and bulk LPG to homes, restaurants, hospitals, and factories across 50+ districts — still with the same rule: deliver safely, on time, and at a fair price.

Est. 1999

Based in Rehmat Shah, Thall — LPG supply from Karak, KPK as well

Brother LPG is located in Rehmat Shah Banda, Thall, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Mujahid Noor & Wahab Noor started the company in 2026 as a cylinder refilling unit for the local community. The job has not changed: fill the bottle safely, send it out on time, and charge a price people can live with.

We also supply LPG from Karak, KPK. Together, Thall and Karak let us reach homes, restaurants, hospitals, and factories across nearby districts with both household cylinders and bulk tanker loads.

Our plant is ISO 9001:2015 certified. The safety record is not a slogan on the wall — it is the standard our founder set, and the one every driver and plant operator is still expected to meet.
